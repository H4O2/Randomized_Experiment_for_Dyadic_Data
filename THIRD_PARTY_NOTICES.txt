Network Repository identifies its dataset terms as Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike. Its policy page does not identify a numbered license
version; no specific version is substituted here. Preserve the source credit,
policy link and any source notices when redistributing the network or derived

This copy preserves the exact input bytes supplied with the paper, including
the single-percent MatrixMarket header. Processing retains all vertices and
undirected edges. It does not add personal identifiers or node attributes.
